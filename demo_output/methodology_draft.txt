Cell type annotation was performed using CellTypePilot (v0.1.0), an evidence-driven annotation pipeline with built-in critic review. Marker gene evidence was sourced from the CellTypePilot Marker Knowledge Graph (MKG mkg-2026.08), a curated atlas integrating PanglaoDB, CellMarker, and Cell Ontology resources. For each of the 8 clusters identified by leiden clustering, marker gene overlap, expression specificity, fold-change magnitude, and negative marker conflict were scored to generate candidate annotations with confidence levels. An independent Annotation Critic module reviewed each assignment for evidence sufficiency, negative marker conflicts, potential doublet signatures, and ontology consistency. Of 8 clusters, 5 were assigned high confidence, 2 medium confidence, and 1 were flagged for manual review. Species: human; tissue context: blood.
