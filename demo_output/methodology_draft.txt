Cell type annotation was performed using CellTypePilot (v0.3.0), an evidence-driven annotation pipeline with built-in critic review. Marker gene evidence was sourced from the CellTypePilot Marker Knowledge Graph (MKG mkg-2026.08), with structured source metadata from PanglaoDB, CellMarker, and Cell Ontology resources. The primary-source-verified marker-edge fraction in scope was 0.0%; database-paper citations were not treated as marker-specific experimental validation. For each of the 8 clusters identified by leiden clustering, positive markers were required to pass direction, adjusted-P, log-fold-change, and within-cluster expression gates; coverage used the complete expected marker panel. A rules-based Annotation Critic reviewed each candidate for evidence sufficiency, negative marker conflicts, potential doublet signatures, and ontology consistency. Candidates with insufficient or conflicting evidence were explicitly reported as Unknown while retaining the best candidate label for human adjudication. No user context pack was applied. Cell states were scored on an independent axis using directional marker evidence; state results were prohibited from changing canonical identity decisions. Confidence categories were rule-based heuristics and were not probability-calibrated. Of 8 clusters, 0 were assigned high confidence, 6 medium confidence, and 8 were flagged for manual review. Species: human; tissue context: blood.
